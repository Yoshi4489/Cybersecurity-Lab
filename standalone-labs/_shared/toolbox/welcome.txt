CYBERSECURITY STANDALONE LAB TOOLBOX
====================================
This shell is for the selected, isolated Docker lab only.
Public networks, the Docker host, and systems outside the lab are out of scope.

Useful commands include nmap, dig, nslookup, curl, jq, ip, ss, nc, find,
grep, sed, awk, strings, base64, sha256sum, and tar.

Run `lab-scope` at any time to review the safety boundary.
